# Build Crew 0.5: the Lab payoff (dump truck, forklift, four blueprints)

Build Crew has no answers, so it has no combo and nothing to grade. What the Lab (lab/SPEC.md section 4.5) gives it is a
reason to keep building: Lab coins, earned in every game, buy two real machines and four new blueprint jobs. Nothing
that was playable is locked: the ten jobs, the sandbox and the skid steer, excavator and crane are exactly as in 0.4
(`tools/physics-tests.mjs` hashes their data against the 0.4.0 source). Everything new is hidden when `/lab/` cannot load.

Code: `src/meta/labBridge.js`, `src/meta/labLooks.js`, `src/blueprint.mjs`, `src/physics.mjs` (`DUMP`, `FORK`, the two
`MACHINE` rows), `src/scene/machines.js` (the two models), `src/content.mjs` (`MACHINES`, `BLUEPRINTS`), `src/app/*` (hooks).

## The Lab plumbing

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| `labBridge.js` | The template of lab/SPEC.md 3.6 with `GAME = 'bc'`: lazy import, queue of 100, `EMPTY` receipts, never throws. One addition: `onReady(fn)`, called when the Lab has loaded (never if it cannot), so Lab buttons and shelves stay hidden until it is really there. |
| `labLooks.js` | `LOOKS_READY` (14 ids: five paints, three decals, two vehicles, four blueprints), `paintPreview(ctx, item, w, h)` for the shop cards, `resolveLook(cosmetics, save)` and `galleryPieces(poses)`. Pure data and canvas, no Three.js. |
| Paint and decal | `labBridge.cosmetics().slots.paint` / `.decal` override `save.paint` / `save.decal` through `applyLook()` (re-read at the start of every round and on every Lab change). Lab paints go through the same `paintMachine` as the job paints; **Checker** is a two-colour check drawn by a patched `MeshStandardMaterial` (`kit.checkerMat`), **Gold** a metallic paint. The three Lab decals (Lab mark, flame, rivets) are extra planes created beside each star and stripe decal, so they sit exactly where those do on all five machines. |
| Hooks | `win()` reports `job` (`meta.first`, `id`, `title`, `pieces`; a replay pays 25 % in the Lab), `piece` (`n` = the blocks the job counted), `blueprint` (101 to 104), `challenge` (once per blueprint visit, when the target is met) and, beside `completeDay`, `day`. The tray's Save site reports `site` (not the quiet save on `pagehide`). `labBridge.beginRound()` runs in `startRound`. No call is made inside a frame. |
| HUD | The Lab pill is a flex item of `.hud-top` (`#lab-hud`), compact on touch. The results strip mounts in the job-complete sheet (`#lab-results`) and is destroyed with the sheet. |
| Title | **The Lab** and **Gallery** (`openHome({ view: 'gallery' })`) buttons, shown only once the Lab has loaded. The Parent Zone's Options tab gets a **The Lab** row (`openParent({ unlocked: true })`). |
| Gallery data | On a job or blueprint completion and on Save site the game passes `meta: { id, title, pieces }`: `id` is `bc.job<n>.<day>` or `bc.site.<day>`, `pieces` at most 48 settled toys as `[typeIndex, colorIndex, x*10, z*10, y*10]` (typeIndex into `Object.keys(TOYS)`, colorIndex into `COLORS`, 255 custom). For a job only the blocks on or beside the pad are sent. The Lab draws plans from the numbers; Build Crew's thumbnails never leave the device. |
| Offline | `prepare-offline.mjs` now writes `SHELL` (the game) and `OPTIONAL` (the 21 Lab modules reachable from `lab/src/index.js`); `sw.js` caches `OPTIONAL` with `Promise.allSettled`, so a missing or blocked Lab can never fail the install. Cache `build-crew-v0.5.0`. |

Unlock rule: `availableMachines()` lists a Lab machine only when `labBridge.owns('bc.veh.<kind>')`; `unlockAll` (the Parent
Zone switch) does not cover them. A blueprint job starts only when `labBridge.owns('bc.bp.<key>')`; otherwise
`startRound` opens the Lab shop. Blueprint ids are accepted by `save.normalize` (101 to 104) and never count in the
"n / 10" on the title or in the Parent Zone report.

## DT-04 dump truck

Real machine, not a skin on the skid steer: the **bed is the tool body**.

| | |
|---|---|
| Controls | Left stick drives. **Lift tips the bed** (I/K or the right stick up and down): `lift` 0.7 to 2.5 m is 0 to 0.92 rad (53 degrees), at 0.62 m/s so a full tip takes about 3 s. No Grab: loads ride in the bed. Voice hint: "Raise the lift to tip the bed. Load it first, then roll forward as the blocks pour out." |
| Feel | Long stopping distance (`ease` 0.022 against the skid steer's 0.12: a stick released at full speed coasts about 2.6 m, the skid steer under 0.5 m), it yaws about its rear axle (front-steer) and turns at 30 % rate at a standstill, never stuck. |
| Bed | Floor 2.78 x 3.6 m, side walls 1.3 m, headboard 1.9 m: an inner width of 2.5 m takes two 1.2 m cubes side by side, six on the floor and more above the walls. Colliders are the model's own boxes (`DUMP`), friction 0.5 so a load stays put while driving and slides out from about 36 degrees. |
| Tailgate | A second kinematic body hinged along the bottom of the rear edge; it drops open into a chute (upper face flush with the floor) between 6 and 20 degrees of tip, well before anything can slide. A first version hung from the top: a 1.2 m cube is taller than the wall, so it caught on the hinge. That is why the bed walls are 1.3 m. |
| Honest physics note | Tipping while standing still leaves a heap against the raised bed, and what rests on the heap stays on the bed. Rolling forward while tipping, as a real driver does, empties it (tested: seven cubes and a beam, bed empty, cubes on the ground). The second hint says so. |
| Model | Ladder frame, twin rear axle on leaf springs, front axle, tank, fenders, a raked-windscreen cab with grille, bumper, lights, mirrors, steps, stack and roof light bar, a steel-floored bed with ribbed walls, a cab shield, a painted tailgate with hinge pins and a telescoping hoist ram that follows the tip. Six instanced wheels (the front pair steer with the turn). Unit plate DT-04, star, stripe and Lab decals on both bed walls and the cab roof, horn on the roof. |

## FL-05 forklift

| | |
|---|---|
| Controls | Left stick drives; **lift** raises the fork tops 0.1 to 3.3 m at 0.9 m/s; **tilt** (J/L, right stick sideways, Tilt buttons) rocks the mast from 3 degrees forward to 11 degrees back. No Grab. Hint: "Lower the forks, line up with a pallet opening and drive in slowly. Then lift." |
| Rear steer | It yaws about the **front axle**, so a turn swings the tail and the forks barely move; the little rear wheels visibly steer against the turn. 50 % turn rate at a standstill. |
| The pallet | A pallet's openings are 1.3 m wide and 0.30 m tall (compound collider in `physics.mjs`, unchanged). The tines are 0.14 m wide at +-0.4 m and 0.07 m thick, 1.7 m long, with a load backrest. Nothing is scripted: an unclean approach really shoves the pallet or rides over it, and the pallet is carried by contact and friction, never by the grab spring. |
| Measured (`physics-tests.mjs`) | Centred, low and level: tines go in, pallet and two cubes rise to 2.7 m. 0.12, -0.15 and 0.2 m off centre still go in (0.2 m is the window). 0.3 and 0.45 m off, yawed 0.08 or 0.15 rad, or forks held at 0.5 or 0.7 m: the pallet is shoved (or ridden over) and never lifted. |
| Coaching | Once a visit, when a pallet is within 4 m ahead: "Lower the forks to the floor before you drive in." (forks high), "Line the forks up with the openings in the pallet." (not lined up), "The forks are in. Lift gently, and tilt back to hold the load." `Yard.forkStatus()` reads it from the real poses. All three are prepared voice lines. |
| Model | Counterbalance truck: rounded counterweight with grille, tail lights and plate, LPG cylinder, operator seat, steering wheel, levers, dash, fenders, an overhead guard with a bar roof, work lights and beacon, mirrors; big front drive wheels and small rear steering wheels; a two-stage mast (outer channels, sliding inner stage that extends once the carriage passes 1.2 m), lift ram, two tilt rams, chains, a carriage with a bar-grid backrest and two tapered tines. The mast and carriage hang from the machine root, so they sit exactly where the physics tool is. |

Both rows exist in all four tables (`MACHINES` content, `MACHINE` physics, `MACHINE` scene, `MACHINE_UI` hud) plus the engine
pitch in `sound.js` (DT-04 44 Hz, FL-05 76 Hz). New table keys: `chassis`, `place`, `gate`, `home`, `liftRate`, `ease`, `pivot`,
`steerLow`, `margin`, `friction`, `tiltRange`, `tiltRate`, `grabs`. The existing rows needed none of them (a test hashes them).
The sandbox now seats up to five machines (`sandboxSpawns`: the Lab machines start in a row behind the others); the 5-machine
cap is in `normalizeYard`. SPEC section 6 said at most three machines; the draw-call and triangle caps still hold with five
(see Budget).

## The four blueprints (jobs 101 to 104)

All four use only the machines everyone has, so a builder who bought a blueprint can always finish it; each is solved by
`tools/solution.mjs` in Node and in the browser.

| # | Blueprint | Price | Machines | Pieces | Challenge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 101 | Lighthouse | 2,400 | crane | slab plinth, three cubes, barrel lamp (top 5.65 m) | height 6.4 m (flag marks it) |
| 102 | Windmill | 2,400 | excavator | three-cube tower, beam sails, door | grabs, par 8 (5 needed) |
| 103 | Castle gate | 3,600 | excavator, crane | two two-cube towers, beam, two merlons | grabs, par 10 (7 needed) |
| 104 | Stone arch | 3,600 | excavator | two barrel-and-cube columns, beam, keystone (top 4.55 m) | height 5.6 m (flag marks it) |

Prices and ids are compared with `lab/src/data/catalog.js` by a Node test. The job board has a **Blueprints** row (locked
tiles show the lock and the price and open the Lab shop; owned ones start) and a **Vehicles** row, both shown only once the Lab has loaded.

**Challenges, never a clock.** The HUD shows a second line in the count: "Grabs 4 · par 8" or "Height 3.6 m · target 6.4 m"
(a tick when met). A grab counts when it picks something up. A height target is met when a settled block's top has
stood at that height at some point (`standingHeight`, from the real poses, orientation included), and it stays met.
Meeting it shows "Neat build!" on the finished sheet and reports `challenge`; missing it changes nothing and says nothing.

Deviation worth knowing: a plain blueprint's ghosts are fixed, so a height target cannot be set by the ghosts themselves.
Each height blueprint therefore ships a spare cube and a target above the finished build: stack the spare on top (on the
barrel or the keystone). The job completes when the ghosts match, so the spare usually goes on afterwards; "Continue on
site" keeps the challenge running and a late success shows a "Neat build!" toast and reports `challenge` once.

## Budget

Measured with `tools/frame-check.mjs` (Chromium + SwiftShader, 64 toys in the sandbox; a third profile buys both Lab machines
through `window.__lab` so the yard holds five). SwiftShader wall-frame times depend on the runner's load and are not a device measurement.

| | 0.4 baseline (3 machines) | 0.5, 3 machines | 0.5, 5 machines (desktop 1024x768) | 0.5, 5 machines (phone 390x844 @3, CPU x4) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Draw calls | 96 | 95 | 124 | 92 (shadows already off at this tier) |
| Triangles | 32,728 | 32,736 | 40,224 | 36,122 |
| p95 CPU update | 9.3 ms | 9.5 ms | 10.7 ms | 23.6 ms (limit 33.3) |
| p95 render | 8.0 ms | 7.0 ms | 11.3 ms | 19.9 ms |
| p95 wall frame (SwiftShader) | | 233 ms | 283 ms | 183 ms (limit 350) |

Each Lab machine costs about 14 draw calls and 3.7k triangles; five machines stay inside the 150 / 120k caps. SPEC section 6
says at most three machines; with the Lab machines bought it is five, and this table is the evidence that the caps still hold.
Real-device frame rates are not measured.

## Phone

The Lab pill is a flex item of the one-line top bar (compact: "Lv n" and a short bar). On a portrait phone (under 470 px)
with the pill the bar takes a second row for the pill and the count (a blueprint's challenge sits in the count's second
line), and the hint strip and side columns move down by that row (`:has(.lab-pill)` rules, so nothing changes without the
Lab). Five machine cards become two short columns. The Grab button is hidden for the truck and forklift. Landscape keeps one row.
`tools/browser-smoke.mjs` still asserts the playfield share, the 43 px targets and the clear title panel.
