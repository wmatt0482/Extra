# Case Files v0.2: the Genesee Valley arc (build contract)

Why: kids open one case a day, and the game has six (Training + Erie 1-5). A child who plays daily has run out. v0.2 adds a
second five-case arc and makes the app arc-aware. SPEC.md and docs/CONTENT-GUIDE.md still hold; this file adds to them.
Every kid rule in SPEC §1 and CONTENT-GUIDE "Kid rules" applies unchanged.

## 1. The arc (content agent)

- Arc `{ id: 'genesee', title: 'The Genesee Valley', blurb, knowledge, targetWords, cases, map: 'genesee' }`, appended to
  `ARCS` after Erie. Add `map: 'erie'` to the Erie arc. Case ids `genesee-01` … `genesee-05`, files in
  `src/content/genesee/`.
- Setting: Rochester and the Genesee River valley (the same town as the Erie arc, so the kids' home ground). Each case may
  sit in a different time; the `setting` line says where and when ("Victor, New York · today", "Rochester, New York ·
  May 1835").
- Every Genesee case has a `town` field, one of exactly: `'rochester'`, `'victor'`, `'letchworth'`, `'charlotte'`
  (Charlotte = the river mouth and lighthouse on Lake Ontario). The route map draws those four places.
- Required topics (order and puzzles are yours):
  1. **Haudenosaunee (Seneca) history and culture, at Ganondagan** (Victor). Set it in the present day at the state historic
     site, with a fictional Seneca educator as a main character, so the Seneca are a living nation today, not a people of
     the past. Good ground: the Three Sisters (corn, beans, squash), the bark longhouse, the Seneca as Keepers of the Western
     Door of the Haudenosaunee Confederacy, the Seneca Art & Culture Center. No conflict, war or destruction story (the
     1687 attack is out of scope for this game), no stereotypes, no costumes-as-props, no invented ceremonies or invented
     words in the Seneca language. Facts only from Ganondagan (ganondagan.org), NYS Parks, the Seneca Nation, the
     Smithsonian NMAI, or a museum; mark anything thin **spot-check**.
  2. **Frederick Douglass or Susan B. Anthony in Rochester** (real people only as `facts`-backed history; e.g. Douglass
     printing The North Star in Rochester from 1847). Kid rules: no "arrest", "jail", "punish" — tell history in words a
     9-year-old can meet safely.
  3-5. Your choice from the valley: the High Falls flour mills ("the Flour City"), the seed and flower nurseries ("the Flower
     City"), Letchworth State Park's gorge and falls, the Charlotte-Genesee lighthouse, the Lilac Festival.
- 8 to 10 `targetWords` for the arc, each met 12+ times (content-tests counts). Arc-level `knowledge` like Erie's.
- Change the Erie finale's `teaser` (erie-05) so it hints at the Genesee arc (no dates). Nothing else in Erie changes.
- `docs/SOURCES.md`: a "Genesee Valley arc" section with every claim and URL. `tools/content-tests.mjs` must pass and must
  cover the new arc (extend it where it hard-codes Erie). Follow CONTENT-GUIDE's recipe and the blind-solve self-check for
  every question.

## 2. The app (app agent)

- Pacing already plays `SEQUENCE` = Training then each arc's cases. Make sure a save that closed all six v0.1 cases (the
  "done" state, "Every case is closed") opens `genesee-01` on the next local day with no parent action, and that nothing
  re-locks or re-opens closed cases. Logic test for exactly that.
- Home: the route map shows **the arc of the current case** (the open or next case; when every case is closed, the last
  arc). Erie keeps today's canal map exactly. Genesee gets its own parchment map in the same style
  (`src/ui/art/routemap.js` grows an arc option, or a sibling file): the Genesee River running north to Lake Ontario,
  Rochester in the middle, Victor to the east, Letchworth to the south, Charlotte at the lake. Stops placed by the case's
  `town`. Same rules: no titles on sealed stops, every stop a 44px button, labels readable at 390px wide.
- Folders: one per arc (already looped). Check the board reads well with two arcs on a phone.
- Lab summary (`labSummary` in main.js): the line names the current arc (`Erie Canal: n / 5`, then `Genesee Valley: n / 5`).
  Never a countdown.
- Anywhere else that hard-codes `erie` (search `src/`): make it arc-aware.
- Tests: logic-tests, ui-smoke (fixtures: add a second small arc to `tools/fixtures` so the home, the map switch and the
  folders are exercised), sync-smoke, content-play (plays every real case; extend to the Genesee cases; it will only pass
  once the content lands — write it, and say so). Version **0.2.0** in sw.js, main.js and the README changelog.

## 3. File ownership

- Content agent: `case-files/src/content/**`, `case-files/docs/SOURCES.md`, `case-files/docs/CONTENT-GUIDE.md`,
  `case-files/tools/content-tests.mjs`, `case-files/tools/content/**`.
- App agent: everything else under `case-files/` (src/ui, src/logic, src/meta, src/main.js, style, sw.js, README, other
  tools, fixtures). Do not edit `src/content/**`.
- Nobody edits other games, `lab/`, or shared `/spell-jump/src/` modules.
